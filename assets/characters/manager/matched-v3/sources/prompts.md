## Daily sheet

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: ONE cohesive production game animation sprite sheet. Use the reference character EXACTLY as the model sheet, NOT a younger shorter chibi redesign. Same face, same large spiral eyes and eye bags, same white-black swept-back hair silhouette, brown rib knit sweater, dark trousers and black loafers. Identical head size, body proportions, drawing style, line weight, palette, and camera in EVERY cell. Important: this repairs a game whose unrelated individual sprites change size and art style when switching. Do not redraw a different face in each pose. ONE transparent sprite sheet, 4 columns by 3 rows of equal SQUARE cells, total landscape aspect 4:3, ideally 2048x1536. No grid lines, no labels, no text, no background, no ground shadows. Each cell has ONE full-body character with ample empty margins. Upright cells all have hair top at 6% cell height and supporting shoe baseline at 95%, fixed vertical height, fixed head size. Standing feet center at 50% cell width. NO independent camera zoom. Front three-quarter view facing screen RIGHT throughout all upright frames, do not reverse direction. Row1 col1: neutral standing identical to reference. Row1 col2: walking cycle frame A right foot forward touching ground, left foot behind, opposite arm swing. Row1 col3: walking cycle frame B passing position, left leg passing forward bent knee, right foot planted under body. Row1 col4: walking cycle frame C left foot forward touching ground, right foot behind, arms reverse frame A. Row2 col1: walking cycle frame D passing position, right leg passing forward bent knee, left foot planted under body. Four walking cells must be a coherent A-B-C-D cycle with head hair torso locked in same registration, feet moving below body, natural short steps, no exaggerated kicks, no spinning, no body shrinking or stretching. Row2 col2: sweeping with both hands holding a broom, broom bristles on ground left of shoes. Row2 col3: same sweeping character, SAME head and torso registration, move only hands and broom slightly to right for second sweep frame. Row2 col4: sitting on a simple low stool, same head size as standing. Row3 col1: sleeping lying on side, same physical head and body size, no bed. Row3 col2: standing looking at smartphone, same head and height. Row3 col3: standing talking with one open hand, same head and height. Row3 col4: standing thinking with finger to chin, same head and height. Preserve EXACT reference identity and adult stocky proportions across the entire sheet. No extra limbs or duplicate characters within cells.

Result: `daily-sheet.png`. The four walking cells were rejected as a cycle; only idle, sweeping, sitting, sleeping, smartphone, talking and thinking are packaged from this sheet. The sheet has imperfect cell registration, so the packaging script records explicit rectangles and shoe anchors rather than assuming a perfect grid. Standing source framing is normalized before shipping; gameplay has no pose-specific scale multipliers.

## Dedicated walking cycle

Use case: identity-preserve. Create ONE production animation sprite sheet of this EXACT landlord walking to the RIGHT, as a true four-frame walk cycle, 2 columns x 2 rows, four equal square cells, transparent background. Same mature chibi proportions, face, spiral eyes, white/black hair, knit brown sweater, dark trousers and loafers as reference. ALL FOUR heads and torsos must be IDENTICAL size, angle and position within the cell. Head top at 8% of cell, ground baseline at 92%, body center x50%, generous transparent gutters, do NOT crop hair or shoes. No grid, text, labels or shadows. Critical: do NOT repeat the same forward leg in both contact frames. Show a slight SIDE VIEW of the walking legs to clearly distinguish the near and far legs. Head remains three-quarter facing right. Frame 1 TOP LEFT: CONTACT: NEAR leg extends forward toward screen RIGHT, FAR leg back to screen LEFT; NEAR arm swings BACK to screen LEFT, FAR arm forward RIGHT. Frame 2 TOP RIGHT: PASSING: NEAR foot planted directly under hips, FAR knee bent and foot lifted passing forward; NEAR arm beside torso. Frame 3 BOTTOM LEFT: OPPOSITE CONTACT: NEAR leg now extends BACK to screen LEFT, FAR leg extends FORWARD screen RIGHT; NEAR arm swings FORWARD to screen RIGHT, FAR arm back LEFT. This pose MUST visually reverse the legs AND arms from frame1, same unchanging head and body. Frame 4 BOTTOM RIGHT: OPPOSITE PASSING: FAR foot planted directly under hips, NEAR knee bent and foot lifted passing forward; NEAR arm beside torso. Natural short steps, loose hands, subtle weary walk. No morphing head/torso, no huge kicking, no turning, no broom or props, no shrinking. Each cell shows the entire character. Same physical height and head size in all four cells. This is an animation with continuity, not four independent illustrations.

Result: `walk-cycle.png`. All four frames use the same packaging scale `455/581`, with head/torso registration and grounded shoe pivots. The in-game cycle advances by actual distance. Preview the actual renderer, both directions and pause/restart, rather than judging the sheet alone.
